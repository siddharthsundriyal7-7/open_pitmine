Grad-CAM Mathematical Formulation

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping calculates neuron importance weights \(\alpha_k^c\) by taking gradients of the target risk score \(y^c\) with respect to feature maps \(A^k\):

$$\alpha_k^c = \frac{1}{Z} \sum_{i} \sum_{j} \frac{\partial y^c}{\partial A_{i,j}^k}$$
$$L_{\text{Grad-CAM}}^c = \text{ReLU}\left(\sum_k \alpha_k^c A^k\right)$$

The resulting activation map generates heat intensity corresponding to pixel regions that directly drove the model to predict High Rockfall Risk.
